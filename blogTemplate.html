Great bread isn't rushed. Time is one of the most important ingredients.

BTBread Tip

Our doughs typically ferment for 18–24 hours before baking, allowing flavour and texture to fully develop.

Benefits of Slow Fermentation

  • More complex flavour
  • Improved texture
  • Better crust development
  • Longer shelf life

The result is bread that tastes richer and stays fresh longer than rapidly-produced loaves.
